Dimaag Tez Hindi Uploader

Publishing tool of the Dimaag Tez Hindi YouTube channel

Dimaag Tez Hindi Uploader is the tool the Dimaag Tez Hindi channel uses to publish its own videos on YouTube. It uploads the channel's videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists, schedules when they go public, and reads how those videos performed.

The tool is used only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public and nobody else signs in to it.

The channel

Dimaag Tez Hindi makes short quiz and learning videos in Hindi: riddles, reasoning, maths tricks, spoken English, technology and money basics.
